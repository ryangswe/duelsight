- A card's name stays on screen more steadily while you point at a moving video, above all on slower computers.
- With "Keep the card I'm pointing at" off, a still card no longer blinks out between checks.
- If card recognition is slow to load, the card under a resting pointer is still named once it's ready.
- After card hovers are switched back on, a background tab whose card recognition was freed loads it when you bring that tab to the front.
- A web page can no longer make DuelSight load card recognition by faking a "video started" signal.
